# Curated Notes: Best YouTube Videos on Writing a Book and Writing a Novel

This is a curated starter guide for two related but different goals:

- `writing a book`: finishing a coherent manuscript from idea to structure
- `writing a novel`: building fiction well at the levels of plot, scene, prose, and world

This is not one official playlist. It is a hand-picked watch path.

Selection principle:

- broad usefulness
- high signal-to-noise
- strong teacher or practitioner credibility
- good coverage of both process and craft

Metrics and links were checked on `2026-04-13`.

## Best Single Full Course

### 1. Brandon Sanderson: Lecture #1: Introduction — Brandon Sanderson on Writing Science Fiction and Fantasy

- Channel: Brandon Sanderson
- Date: 2020-01-22
- Duration: 1:03:52
- Views: 2,410,415
- URL: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-6HOdHEeosc>

Why it is here:

- If you want one serious long-form starting point, this is the strongest entry.
- It is fiction-focused, but the advice on story, character, structure, and professional practice travels well beyond fantasy.
- Treat this as the gateway into Sanderson's broader lecture series, not just a one-off video.

## Recommended Watch Order

If you want the shortest useful path:

1. `How to Write a Book: 13 Steps From a Bestselling Author`
2. `How to Write a Novel: My Proven 12-Step Process`
3. `Five Tips for Writing Your First Novel`
4. `How to Show, Not Tell: The Complete Writing Guide`

If you want the stronger long-form path:

1. `Lecture #1: Introduction — Brandon Sanderson on Writing Science Fiction and Fantasy`
2. `How to Write a Novel: My Proven 12-Step Process`
3. `How to Show, Not Tell: The Complete Writing Guide`
4. `How to write descriptively`
5. `How to build a fictional world`

## What I Would Actually Tell You to Do

If your goal is `finish a book`, start with Jerry B. Jenkins.

If your goal is `become a better novelist`, start with Brandon Sanderson and Diane Callahan.

If your goal is `write clearer sentences`, add Steven Pinker early.
