# Recommended One Open Video Course For Almost Anyone

If I had to recommend exactly one open video course for almost anyone on Earth, I would pick:

## Michael Sandel: Justice

Subtitle: `What's the Right Thing to Do?`

- Official course page: <https://justiceharvard.org/justicecourse/>
- YouTube playlist: <https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL30C13C91CFFEFEA6>

## Why this one

- It has almost no technical prerequisites.
- It improves judgment rather than training for only one profession.
- It forces you to think clearly about fairness, rights, duty, markets, merit, citizenship, and the common good.
- It stays useful whether you become an engineer, scientist, entrepreneur, teacher, parent, civil servant, or artist.
- It is one of the rare courses that is both serious and broadly accessible.

## Recommendation

If someone can only do one course, I would rather they learn how to reason about justice, obligation, and human life than learn one narrow technical skill too early.
